# 5e Rules Reference (DM quick sheet)

Written for D&D 5e; where the 2014 and 2024 rules differ, both are noted.
Ask Mug once per campaign which edition the table uses and record it.

## Core roll

d20 + ability modifier (+ proficiency bonus if proficient) vs DC / AC.
- **Ability modifier** = (score − 10) / 2, round down.
- **Proficiency bonus**: lvl 1–4 +2, 5–8 +3, 9–12 +4, 13–16 +5, 17–20 +6.
- **Advantage/disadvantage**: roll 2d20, take higher/lower. Any number of
  sources cancel to a straight roll. They don't stack.
- **Nat 20 / nat 1**: automatic hit/miss on attacks only (and death saves).
  Not on skill checks RAW (2024: nat 20 on a d20 test is not auto-success
  for checks either). House-rule if the table wants.
- **Passive score** = 10 + modifiers (+5 adv / −5 disadv). Passive
  Perception notices hidden things without a roll.

Group check: everyone rolls; succeed if at least half succeed.
Contest: both roll, higher wins; tie keeps status quo.
Help action: one ally gives advantage.

## Skills by ability

- **STR**: Athletics
- **DEX**: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth
- **INT**: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion
- **WIS**: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival
- **CHA**: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion
- CON has no skills (concentration, endurance, holding breath).

## Combat sequence

1. Determine surprise (2014: surprised creatures can't act/react on round 1.
   2024: surprised creatures roll initiative with disadvantage).
2. Positions. 3. Initiative: d20 + DEX (ties: higher DEX, then players pick).
4. Rounds of 6 seconds; each creature takes a turn.

**On a turn**: Movement (speed, can split around action) + one Action +
one Bonus Action (only if something grants one) + free object interaction.
One Reaction per round (resets at start of your turn).

**Actions**: Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash (extra move = speed), Disengage (no
opportunity attacks), Dodge (attacks vs you at disadv, DEX saves at adv),
Help, Hide (Stealth vs passive Perception), Ready (trigger + reaction),
Search, Use an Object. 2024 adds Influence, Study, Utilize as named actions.

**Attack roll**: d20 + ability mod + proficiency (+ magic) vs AC.
- Melee: STR (finesse may use DEX). Ranged: DEX. Spell: casting ability.
- **Crit**: double the damage *dice*, not modifiers.
- Ranged attack with a hostile within 5 ft: disadvantage. Beyond normal
  range up to long range: disadvantage.
- **Opportunity attack**: when a hostile leaves your reach willingly.
- **Two-weapon fighting**: light weapon in each hand; bonus action attack
  with off-hand, no ability mod to damage unless negative (2024: Light
  property, Nick mastery changes this).
- **Spell save DC** = 8 + proficiency + casting mod. **Spell attack** =
  proficiency + casting mod.

**Cover**: half +2 AC & DEX saves; three-quarters +5; total = can't be
targeted directly.

**Unseen**: attacking an unseen target = disadvantage; attacker unseen =
advantage. Prone: melee within 5 ft has adv vs it, ranged has disadv; standing
up costs half speed.

**Grapple / Shove**
- 2014: Athletics vs target's Athletics or Acrobatics; replaces one attack.
- 2024: Unarmed Strike option; target makes STR or DEX save vs DC 8 + STR
  mod + proficiency.
- Grappled: speed 0. Escape uses an action.

**Mounted, underwater, falling**
- Falling: 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 ft, max 20d6; land prone.
- Underwater: melee without swim speed at disadv unless dagger/javelin/
  shortsword/spear/trident; ranged beyond normal range auto-miss.
- Suffocation: hold breath 1 + CON mod minutes (min 30 s); then CON mod
  rounds (min 1), then drop to 0 HP.

## Damage, dying, death

- **Damage types**: acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, force, lightning,
  necrotic, piercing, poison, psychic, radiant, slashing, thunder.
  Resistance = half, vulnerability = double, immunity = none.
- At **0 HP**: unconscious, death saves each turn: d20, 10+ success, <10
  fail; 3 successes = stable, 3 fails = dead. Nat 20 → regain 1 HP. Nat 1 →
  two failures. Damage while at 0 = one failure (crit = two).
- **Instant death**: remaining damage after reaching 0 ≥ max HP.
- Stabilize: Medicine DC 10 (action) or healer's kit.
- Stable creature regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours.
- **Temp HP** don't stack; take the higher. Lost first.
- **Massive damage** (optional): ≥ half max HP in one hit → CON DC 15.

## Conditions (short)

- **Blinded**: auto-fail sight checks; attacks vs it adv; its attacks disadv.
- **Charmed**: can't harm charmer; charmer has adv on social checks vs it.
- **Deafened**: auto-fail hearing checks.
- **Exhaustion**: 2014 — 6 levels: 1 disadv checks; 2 speed halved; 3
  disadv attacks & saves; 4 HP max halved; 5 speed 0; 6 death. 2024 — each
  level −2 to d20 tests and −5 ft speed; 6 = death. Long rest removes 1.
- **Frightened**: disadv on checks/attacks while source in sight; can't
  move closer.
- **Grappled**: speed 0.
- **Incapacitated**: no actions or reactions (2024: also no bonus actions,
  concentration broken, can't speak).
- **Invisible**: unseen; adv on its attacks, disadv on attacks vs it.
- **Paralyzed**: incapacitated, can't move/speak; auto-fail STR/DEX saves;
  attacks vs it adv; hits within 5 ft are crits.
- **Petrified**: stone; resist all damage; immune poison/disease.
- **Poisoned**: disadv on attacks and ability checks.
- **Prone**: crawl; disadv on attacks; see above for attacks vs it.
- **Restrained**: speed 0; attacks vs it adv; its attacks disadv; disadv
  DEX saves.
- **Stunned**: incapacitated; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks vs it adv.
- **Unconscious**: incapacitated, prone, drops items; auto-fail STR/DEX
  saves; attacks vs it adv; hits within 5 ft are crits.

## Concentration

One concentration spell at a time. On taking damage: CON save DC 10 or half
the damage, whichever is higher (2024: max DC 30). Broken by incapacitation
or death. Track it on the combat sheet — it's the most-forgotten rule.

## Spellcasting essentials

- Components: V (speak), S (free hand), M (focus/pouch unless costly or
  consumed).
- **Bonus-action spell rule (2014)**: if you cast a bonus-action spell, the
  only other spell that turn is a cantrip with casting time 1 action. (2024:
  only one spell slot spent per turn.)
- Rituals: +10 minutes, no slot, needs ritual tag + feature.
- Upcasting: higher slot for listed bonus.
- Counterspell / Dispel: 3rd-level auto vs ≤3rd; higher needs ability check
  DC 10 + spell level (2024 Counterspell: target makes CON save).
- Areas: count squares the template touches; at the table, generous
  "how many are bunched up" is fine.

## Resting

- **Short rest**: 1 hour; spend Hit Dice (roll + CON mod each) to heal.
  Recharges short-rest features (warlock slots, Action Surge, Ki/Focus, etc.).
- **Long rest**: 8 hours (≤2 h light activity), once per 24 h, need 1 HP.
  Regain all HP and spent Hit Dice up to half your total (2024: all Hit
  Dice). Interrupted by 1 h of walking/fighting/casting (2024: any combat).

## Exploration & travel

| Pace | Per hour | Per day | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast | 4 mi | 30 mi | −5 passive Perception |
| Normal | 3 mi | 24 mi | — |
| Slow | 2 mi | 18 mi | can Stealth |

Forced march: past 8 h, CON save DC 10 + 1 per extra hour or 1 exhaustion.
Difficult terrain halves speed. Food: 1 lb/day; water 1 gal/day (2 hot).
Light: bright, dim (disadv Perception sight), darkness (blinded).
Darkvision: dim as bright, dark as dim within range — no colour.

## Social

Attitude: Hostile / Indifferent / Friendly. Persuasion/Deception/
Intimidation shift willingness, not mind control. A good argument can lower
the DC or grant advantage. Insight vs Deception for lies.

Multiply monster XP by: 1 monster ×1, 2 ×1.5, 3–6 ×2, 7–10 ×2.5,
11–14 ×3, 15+ ×4. Adjust a step for parties under 3 or over 5.

L2 100/150/200, L3 150/225/400, L4 250/375/500, L5 500/750/1100,

5=1800, 6=2300, 7=2900, 8=3900, 9=5000, 10=5900, 11=7200, 12=8400,

**Adventuring day**: ~6–8 medium encounters with 2 short rests (2014).
Most tables run 2–4 bigger fights — that's fine, make each one matter.

## Treasure & economy rough guide

1 pp = 10 gp = 100 sp = 1000 cp. Commoner day wage 1 sp; skilled 2 gp.
Inn (modest) 5 sp/night; meal 3 sp; ale 4 cp; healing potion 50 gp
(2d4+2). Magic item rarity by tier: common/uncommon at 1–4, rare from ~5,
very rare from ~11, legendary from ~17. Attunement max 3.
