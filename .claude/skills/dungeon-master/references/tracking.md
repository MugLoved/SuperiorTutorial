# What a DM Tracks

A DM juggles four timescales. I keep all of them in files so nothing drops.

## 1. Moment-to-moment (live, every exchange)

- Current scene: location, time of day, light, weather, who is present.
- Marching order and who is holding the torch / watching the door.
- PC HP, temp HP, conditions, concentration, exhaustion.
- Expended resources: spell slots, class features (Rage, Ki/Focus, Channel
  Divinity, Action Surge, Bardic Inspiration, Wild Shape, Lay on Hands
  pool), Hit Dice, consumables, ammunition (recover half after a fight),
  rations, torches/oil, charges on items.
- Inspiration / Heroic Inspiration held.
- Noise and alarm level ("did that wake the barracks?").
- What the players *think* is true vs what *is* true.

## 2. Per session

- Scenes visited, choices made, rolls that mattered (crits, nat 1s,
  clutch saves — these become table legends; reference them later).
- NPCs met (new names!), their attitude now, what they were promised.
- Loot found and who took it; money split.
- XP or milestone progress.
- Clues found / missed. If a crucial clue was missed, note where it can
  resurface (Three Clue Rule: every conclusion gets 3 clues).
- Spotlight balance: which player got little focus? Give them the next hook.
- Spontaneous events that fired and whether they were played or banked.
- Real-world: who attended, who was absent and how their PC is explained.

## 3. Per arc / campaign

- **Front & clocks**: each threat/faction has a goal and a clock (4, 6 or
  8 segments). Tick on time passing, player failures, or ignored hooks.
  When full, the bad thing happens — in the world, visibly.
- **Threads**: open questions/hooks, who introduced them, status
  (open / chasing / resolved / abandoned). Abandoned threads don't vanish;
  they resolve offscreen, often badly.
- **Factions**: goal, resources, leader, attitude to party (−3 to +3),
  what they want from the party.
- **Calendar**: in-world date, moon phase, holidays, deadlines, travel
  days elapsed. Time is a resource — show it passing.
- **Geography**: places visited, distances, what changed since last visit.
- **Consequences ledger**: debts, enemies made, crimes witnessed, favours
  owed, lies told (players forget lies; the world doesn't).
- **Reputation** per town/faction.
- **House rules & rulings** log (consistency is fairness).
- **Edition** in use (2014 vs 2024), leveling method, optional rules
  (flanking, crit rules, massive damage, encumbrance).

## 4. Per PC

- Name, player, race/species, class/subclass, level, background.
- AC, max HP, passive Perception/Insight/Investigation, speed, senses,
  saves, notable skills, spell save DC, languages.
- **Bonds, flaws, ideals, goals, secrets** — and one personal hook per PC
  being woven into the main story.
- Backstory NPCs (family, rivals, mentors) to bring in.
- Attuned items, notable gear, curses, diseases, oaths, debts.
- How the player likes to play (combat / roleplay / exploration / chaos).

## 5. Safety & table

- Lines (never on screen) and Veils (happens offscreen / fade to black).
- Player content notes, phobias.
- Session length, breaks, snack order if it matters to the plot (it
  always does).

## How I log

- `campaign.md`: bible — rarely changes, updated at session close.
- `party.md`: PC blocks; live values updated at pauses.
- `npcs.md`: one line per NPC minimum: `Name — role, location, want / fear
  / tic / secret, attitude, last seen session N`.
- `sessions/NN.md`: from `templates/session.md`, written live in bullets,
  summary at the end.
- Combat: `templates/combat.md` pasted into chat each round; final state
  saved into the session log.

Rule: if it was said out loud at the table and could matter later, it gets
written down. Names first.
